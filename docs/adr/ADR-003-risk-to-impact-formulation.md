# ADR-003: Risk-to-Impact Prioritization Formulation & Explainability

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Raw Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTCs) and threshold triggers overload fleet managers with "alert fatigue." In a 100,000-vehicle fleet, thousands of minor fault codes appear daily (e.g. ambient temperature sensor warnings or tire pressure variance). Alerting on raw counts forces dispatchers to spend critical hours triaging low-severity noise while severe impending mechanical failures go unnoticed until roadside breakdown occurs.

## Decision
We define an explicit, mathematically sound, and operationally grounded **Priority Scoring Model**:

$$\text{PriorityScore} = \text{RiskProbability} \times \text{ImpactExposure} \times \text{UrgencyFactor}$$

Where:
1. **RiskProbability ($P \in [0.0, 1.0]$)**:
   - Estimated failure probability over the next forecast horizon (48 hours / 500 km).
   - Generated by our Tabular Gradient-Boosted Model (with an explainable deterministic baseline as fallback and benchmark).
   - Evaluated on features: active DTC severity weights, rolling temperature slope, harsh braking frequency, lifetime odometer vs. maintenance interval, and battery health degradation.

2. **ImpactExposure ($I \in [1.0, 10.0]$)**:
   - Estimated operational loss if the vehicle stalls or is grounded unscheduled.
   - Calculated as:
     $$I = w_{\text{class}} \cdot \text{VehicleClassWeight} + w_{\text{util}} \cdot \text{CurrentTripUtilization} + w_{\text{rev}} \cdot \text{RouteRevenueTier}$$
   - Example: A fully loaded refrigerated freight truck on a cross-country route has $I \approx 9.5$, whereas an empty depot shuttle has $I \approx 1.8$.

3. **UrgencyFactor ($U \in [1.0, 5.0]$)**:
   - Time-to-failure proximity modifier based on rate-of-change physics:
     - Critical thermal runaway or safety-critical brake loss: $U = 5.0$ (immediate grounding).
     - Moderate cooling degradation or high-mileage wear: $U = 2.0$ (service within 3 days).
     - Non-critical sensor drift: $U = 1.0$ (next scheduled cycle).

### Explainability Requirement
Every score emitted by FleetPulse must include a structured factor breakdown:
```json
{
  "priority_score": 87.4,
  "risk_probability": 0.82,
  "impact_exposure": 7.8,
  "urgency_factor": 4.0,
  "contributing_factors": [
    {"factor": "Coolant Temp Spike Rate", "weight": 0.38, "value": "+1.4 C/min", "threshold": "+0.5 C/min"},
    {"factor": "DTC Severity (P0128, P0300)", "weight": 0.32, "detail": "Engine Misfire & Coolant Thermostat Malfunction"},
    {"factor": "Overdue Maintenance Interval", "weight": 0.18, "value": "14,200 km since last service", "threshold": "10,000 km"},
    {"factor": "Route Utilization Tier", "weight": 0.12, "detail": "High-priority long-haul route"}
  ]
}
```

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Eliminates alert fatigue; fleet managers receive a ranked queue sorted by actionable business impact.
- **Negative**: Requires careful parameter calibration, validated against simulated ground truth and verified by the baseline model.
